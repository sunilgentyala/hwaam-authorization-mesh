Architecture

A shared decision model with distributed enforcement.

Decision inputs

Principal chain

Human, workload, agent, sub-agent, and tool identities remain distinct.

Resource

Authorization is bound to an object, type, owner tenant, and resource attributes.

Mission envelope

Purpose is converted into concrete action, resource, impact, time, output, and delegation limits.

Policy bundle

Roles, attributes, relationships, approvals, risk limits, and obligations are versioned.

Decision outcomes

HWAAM returns allow, deny, or conditional_allow. Conditional decisions contain obligations such as approval, step-up authentication, session recording, or restricted output.

Security invariants

  1. Delegation cannot expand effective authority.
  2. Tenant boundaries remain closed unless explicitly reviewed.
  3. Every action remains inside the mission.
  4. Delegation depth is bounded.
  5. Protected operations use an approved enforcement point.
  6. Revocation freshness is bounded.
  7. Sensitive decisions produce complete evidence.
  8. Missing security facts cause denial.
